RULES

The rules
of the game

A summary of the essential rules for the 1st Edition published so far. For any question, write to ignacio@musubigara.org.

Essential rules

  • FormatA single non-stop stage of 214 km and 6,020 m D+.
  • Self-sufficiencyNo assistance and no conventional aid stations: each runner carries what they need.
  • Zero WasteDropping a single piece of packaging on the course means immediate disqualification and a ban from future editions. It is the only rule with no appeal.
  • NavigationOfficial GPS track is mandatory, with stamping at all 6 checkpoints.
  • Time limit50 hours from the start, with the control closing at 18:00.
  • Entry300 places, with pre-registration and a mandatory motivation letter. Registration opens in January 2027.
  • SafetyGPS tracker with SOS button, a control centre in Vitoria-Gasteiz and specific ultra-trail insurance with rescue cover, including helicopter, plus international civil liability.
  • Image rightsRunners grant image rights for editorial and promotional use of the event; direct commercial use requires individual written permission.

The fee and the registration process are explained in the FAQ.
