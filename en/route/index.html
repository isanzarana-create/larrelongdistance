ROUTE

The 214 km
route

LARRE Long Distance ® is a non-stop ultra-trail: a single-stage loop that starts and finishes in Vitoria-Gasteiz and crosses the four Álavas.

Route facts

  • Distance214 km
  • Elevation gain6,020 m
  • FormatSingle stage, non-stop loop
  • Start and finishPlaza de los Fueros, Vitoria-Gasteiz
  • Start time16:00
  • Time limit50 hours (control closes at 18:00)
  • AreasEntzia, Toloño, Cantabria and Izki ranges, and Rioja Alavesa
  • Places300 runners

Stamping checkpoints

The route has 6 stamping checkpoints. They are not aid stations: LARRE is run self-supported. Points follow the planned route.

PointTownKilometre
StartVitoria-Gasteiz0

Navigation

The official GPS track is mandatory. Every runner carries a GPS tracker with an SOS button, and the organisation runs a control centre in Vitoria-Gasteiz.
