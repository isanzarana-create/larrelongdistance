Getting to
the start

Ultra Trail Larre® starts and finishes in the same place: Parque de la Florida, in the centre of Vitoria-Gasteiz, capital of the Basque Country.

Start and finish

Place
Parque de la Florida
City

Finish
The same park, up to 50 hours after the start

See Parque de la Florida on Google Maps

Getting to Vitoria-Gasteiz

By train or bus: the city has a railway station and a bus station, both a few minutes from the centre.

By plane: Vitoria-Gasteiz has its own airport (Foronda). Bilbao airport is a little over an hour away by road.

By car: the city is well connected by motorway with Bilbao, Donostia-San Sebastián, Pamplona and Burgos.

We promote train over car: it is one of the rules of the Zero Code.
