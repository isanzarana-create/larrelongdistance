Get on
the list

300 places in order of arrival. Tell us who you are in a few lines: without a description the pre-registration is not validated, but it does not decide your place.

How it works: you fill in the form, you receive an email and confirm your address with a link (double confirmation). Order of arrival is set when you submit the form; only pre-registrations confirmed within 7 days count. Registrations open in January 2027.

The pre-registration list opens on 1 December 2026. Until then the form stays closed.

Between 300 and 600 characters: who you are, where you come from as a runner and why you want to be on this start line.
Events, routes or crossings you would like to mention.

Processors: Netlify (hosting and forms) and Resend (email delivery). No disclosure to third parties except where legally required.

Access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction, portability and withdrawal of consent, by writing to ignacio@musubigara.org.
