The essentials of the 1st Edition rules, on one page.

Essential rules

Format
A single non-stop stage of 226.9 km and 4,644 m D+.
Self-sufficiency
No assistance and no classic aid stations: each runner carries what they need.
Navigation
Official GPS track required, and stamping at the 6 checkpoints.
Time limit
50 hours from the start, with the control closing at 18:00.
Participation
300 places in order of arrival of the pre-registration, which includes a mandatory short personal description. Registrations in January 2027.
Safety
GPS tracker with SOS button, a control centre in Vitoria-Gasteiz and specific ultra-trail insurance covering rescue, helicopter included, and international civil liability.
Image rights
The runner grants image rights for editorial and promotional use of the event; direct commercial use requires individual written authorisation.
Mandatory gear
To be published with the final rules of the 1st Edition.

Zero Code · 10 rules

Dropping a single piece of litter on the course means immediate disqualification and a ban from future editions. It is the only rule with no appeal.

  1. Zero waste in the mountains. All litter leaves in the runner's pack until the next inhabited place.Disqualification
  2. Reusable containers required. Flasks or soft flasks. Single-use bottles are banned from the starting kit.Disqualification
  3. Gels and bars: wrapper in your pack. Every wrapper that goes in must come out. Checked at the finish and at checkpoints.Disqualification
  4. No going off-track. You may not leave the route except on authorised diversions: not for a photo, a shortcut or a bivouac.Disqualification
  5. Bivouac without a trace. If you rest in the mountains, you leave no sign. No fires. No visible organic waste.Disqualification
  6. Reach the event by public transport. We promote train over car.Commitment
  7. Reusable pack, no single-use plastic. Organic cotton, FSC card, recycled technical fabric. No blister packs, no individual bags.Production
  8. Trackers on perpetual rental. The electronics fleet is rented year after year. We do not buy disposable GPS units. Straps and cases are reused.Production
  9. Finish-line food at km 0. At least 70% local produce from within 30 km of the route. No individual packaging.Production
  10. Annual public audit. The event's footprint (transport, production, logistics) is calculated and published. Real, measured reduction comes first, with symbolic offsetting of residual emissions through Retree the Planet.Transparency
