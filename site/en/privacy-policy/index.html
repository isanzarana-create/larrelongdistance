policy

What data we collect when you pre-register, what for, for how long and what rights you have. Version 2026-11-v1.

1. Data controller

  • Controller: Ignacio Sanz Arana
  • Tax ID (NIF): 72752567-W
  • Postal address: Pablo Uranga 10, 01008 Vitoria-Gasteiz (Álava), Spain
  • Contact: ignacio@musubigara.org

2. Data we process

  • Pre-registration form data: full name, email, country of residence, personal description, experience (optional), language, and your choice about receiving news.
  • Record data: date and time of submission and confirmation, order number, version of the consent text you accepted.
  • Technical data: IP address and browser, which the form provider logs on submission for security and anti-spam reasons.

We do not ask for special categories of data. Please do not include them in your description.

3. Purposes and legal basis

  • Managing your pre-registration: validating your email (double confirmation), assigning the order-of-arrival number and contacting you about the opening of registrations. Basis: your consent (art. 6.1.a GDPR) and pre-contractual measures at your request (art. 6.1.b).
  • Sending you Ultra Trail Larre® news, only if you tick the optional box. Basis: your consent (art. 6.1.a). You can withdraw it at any time without affecting the above.
  • Security and prevention of form abuse. Basis: legitimate interest (art. 6.1.f).

No automated decisions or profiling take place. The personal description is mandatory but does not determine your place: that is set by the order of arrival of confirmed pre-registrations.

4. Retention periods

  • Unconfirmed pre-registrations: deleted after 30 days.
  • Confirmed pre-registrations: until the 1st Edition registration process ends; they are then deleted, unless you register, in which case the registration conditions apply.
  • News subscription: until you unsubscribe.
  • Data may be kept blocked for the limitation periods of possible legal liabilities.

5. Recipients and processors

We do not disclose your data to third parties except where legally required. To provide the service we use the following processors, with whom the corresponding data processing agreement (art. 28 GDPR) is in place:

  • Netlify, Inc.: website hosting, form collection and running the functions that handle pre-registration.
  • Resend, Inc.: sending the confirmation email.

6. International transfers

Both providers are established in the United States. Transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified and, failing that, on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. You may ask us for more information about the safeguards applied.

7. Your rights

You may exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and portability, and withdraw your consent, by writing to ignacio@musubigara.org and stating the email you pre-registered with. We will reply within one month.

If you believe we have not processed your data in line with the rules, you may lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).

8. Cookies and tracking

This site uses no first-party or third-party cookies, and no analytics or advertising tools. Fonts are served from the site itself.

9. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures: encrypted connection (HTTPS), signed confirmation links with an expiry, restricted access to data and periodic deletion of unconfirmed data.

10. Changes

If this policy changes materially, a new version and date will be published here. The version of the text you accept is recorded with your pre-registration.
