The 226.9 km
route

Ultra Trail Larre® is a non-stop ultra-trail: a single-stage loop that starts and finishes at Parque de la Florida in Vitoria-Gasteiz and crosses the four Álavas.

Stamping checkpoints

The route includes 6 stamping checkpoints. They are not aid stations: Ultra Trail Larre is run self-supported. Their locations will be confirmed before registrations open.

Navigation

The official GPS track is mandatory. Every runner carries a GPS tracker with an SOS button, and the organisation runs a control centre in Vitoria-Gasteiz.

How to plan water, food, sleep and gear on a route like this is explained in the self-supported ultra guide.
