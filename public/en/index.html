01The race

One start, one finish and the mountains of Álava in between. What happens between the two is up to you.

Self-supported

Ultra Trail Larre® is a self-supported ultra: no outside assistance and no organiser aid stations. You decide what to carry, when to eat, when to sleep and how to manage your water. You prepare for it like an expedition, not like a circuit.

Just you, a tracker and your brevet

A tracker follows you for the whole race, and your brevet records your passage through the controls. Navigation is part of the challenge.

Solo or relay

You can run it all by yourself or as a relay. The details of the relay option will be published later.

Philosophy

It is not a mass-participation race and does not want to be. There are 300 places, and that number is an identity, not a market parameter. For many people, the challenge will simply be to finish.

The mountains

We cross ranges, villages and vineyards of Álava where people live all year round. We pass through with respect and leave no trace: that is the condition for coming back.

Is it for you?

Ultra Trail Larre® is not a beginner’s race. It asks for previous long-distance experience, a cool head in the small hours and acceptance of the Zero Waste Code. If that sounds more like a privilege than a sacrifice, this is your race.

03Zero Waste Code

What goes into the mountains with you comes out of the mountains with you. The only trace we leave is our footprints.

Your bag

Your own rubbish bag in a fixed pocket, always the same one.

Every wrapper

Gel and bar tabs tied or pocketed the moment you open them, never “later”.

Before the start

Decant what you can into reusable bags so you carry less packaging from the beginning.

The conditions of the Zero Waste Code are in the rules.

07FAQ

The essentials, plainly. If something is missing, write to us.

When is the race?

The first edition takes place in autumn 2027. We have not published the exact day yet.

Where are the start and the finish?

At the bandstand in La Florida Park, Vitoria-Gasteiz. The route is a loop: you start and finish in the same place.

How long is it and how much climbing?

227 km and +5,290 m of elevation gain, self-supported.

When does pre-registration open?

The pre-registration list opens on 1 December 2026.

How are the 300 places allocated?

In order of pre-registration. The short personal description is compulsory: it validates your pre-registration and does not filter applicants. There is no bib reservation and no motivation letter.

Does pre-registering guarantee a place?

No. When you pre-register you receive a reference code by email, and we will email you the payment date in March.

What data do you ask for?

First name, surname, age, sex, email, previous races, whether you are a member of a mountaineering federation, a short personal description and, if you wish, comments. We will ask for your ID number when you confirm your place.

Can it be run as a relay?

Yes. Besides running it whole on your own, there is the option to run it as a relay. The details of this option will be published later.

How do the controls work?

There are six controls. Four are confirmed (Agurain, Santa Cruz de Campezo, Laguardia and Peñacerrada) and the other two are still to be defined. A tracker follows you throughout the race and your brevet records your passage.

What is the Zero Waste Code?

What goes into the mountains with you comes out with you. It is set out in the race rules.

Where can I read the rules?

In this PDF, with the essential rules of the first edition.
