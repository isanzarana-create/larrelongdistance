Race rules

Race rules

Last updated: 23 November 2026

1. Purpose and scope

These rules govern participation in Ultra Trail Larre®, 1st Edition (29 to 31 October 2027), and bind the organiser and everyone who registers. Pre-registering does not imply acceptance of these rules: express acceptance happens when you complete registration, with payment, in the phase that opens on 2 January 2027. These rules prevail over any earlier information published on the website, on social media or in informal communications from the organiser.

2. Organiser

Ultra Trail Larre® is organised by Ignacio Sanz Arana (Tax ID 72752567W), with an address at Pintor Pablo Uranga 12, 01008, Vitoria-Gasteiz (Álava), Spain, under the Musubigara® brand. Every reference to “the organiser” in these rules means Ignacio Sanz Arana. The official contact for the organiser is ignacio@musubigara.org.

3. Edition, date, venue and categories

The 1st Edition takes place from 29 to 31 October 2027, starting and finishing at the kiosk in Parque de La Florida, Vitoria-Gasteiz (Álava). The organiser will announce the exact start time with enough notice before registration.

You can take part solo, running the full route on your own, or as a relay, in teams of 3 or 4 who split the route however they decide, with a single bib and a single tracker per team passed from one runner to the next. In both categories, going through every control on the route, in the official order and direction, is compulsory.

4. The route and the controls

The route is a 227 km loop with 5,590 m of positive elevation, following the official track published by the organiser (GPX available on the website). Any change to the route for reasons beyond the organiser’s control (roadworks, weather, administrative authorisation, safety) will be announced and will not give rise to a claim or refund where it does not substantially change the nature of the race.

There are six controls, checked through each runner’s or team’s tracker and brevet. Runners are responsible for going through every control; failing to do so may result in disqualification, under Article 9.

5. Self-sufficiency and gear

Ultra Trail Larre® is a self-supported race: the organiser provides no aid stations or outside assistance on the course, beyond marking, the controls and tracker-based monitoring. Each runner decides and carries what they need to finish the race — food, water, warm clothing, lighting, and navigation and safety gear — and is solely responsible for that decision.

The organiser may publish a list of mandatory or recommended gear, with reasonable notice before registration opens. If published, it becomes part of these rules, and failing to carry it may be sanctioned under Article 9.

6. Eligibility to take part

To take part you must: (a) be 18 years old on the date of the race; (b) have sufficient fitness and prior experience in long-distance mountain races — Ultra Trail Larre® is not a beginners’ race; (c) expressly accept these rules and the Zero Waste Code (Article 13) when completing registration; and (d) declare, on your own responsibility, that you have no medical condition that prevents or advises against taking part, and that you hold the insurance referred to in Article 11.

The organiser may refuse or cancel a registration where it finds these requirements are not met. Where the failure is attributable to the participant — for example, giving false information — cancellation does not give rise to a refund.

7. Pre-registration and registration

The process has two phases. Pre-registration, with no payment, opens on 1 December 2026 at 14:00 (CET) and includes a short, mandatory personal description, which is checked but does not filter applicants; there is no bib reservation and no motivation letter. Pre-registration does not guarantee a place: the 300 places are allocated in order of arrival of the pre-registration. Registration, with payment, opens on 2 January 2027; your place is confirmed once you pay.

The entry fee is €185 for solo runners and €135 per participant for relay teams (of 3 or 4 people). The fee does not include insurance: see Article 11. An ID document is requested once your place is confirmed.

8. Relay teams

Each member of a relay team registers individually and must individually meet the requirements of Article 6. The team is free to organise how it splits the route, with a single bib and a single tracker handed from one runner to the next; the only requirement is that the team, between them, goes through every control.

Whoever manages the team’s registration acts as the point of contact with the organiser. Each member is individually responsible for complying with these rules; a serious breach by one member may affect the whole team’s result or disqualification, under Article 9.

9. Time limit and disqualification

The time limit to finish the route and appear in the results is 60 hours from the start. After that, the participant or team is unranked; they may choose to continue at their own risk, letting the organiser know.

Grounds for disqualification include: not going through a control; exceeding the time limit; receiving outside assistance not allowed under the self-sufficiency rules; a serious breach of the Zero Waste Code or of the conduct rules in these regulations; and falsifying registration data or control records. The decision rests with the organiser, following the procedure in Article 18.

10. Withdrawing during the race

A participant who decides to withdraw must tell the organiser or the nearest control as soon as possible, to avoid triggering an unnecessary search. The organiser does not provide rescue or evacuation services: in an emergency, the public emergency services are called (112). Unless emergency regulations say otherwise, the cost of any transport or evacuation is the participant’s own.

11. Health and insurance

Each participant must hold, at their own expense, valid accident and civil liability insurance covering long-distance mountain racing — for example through a mountaineering federation licence or an equivalent policy — for the whole race, and must declare it during registration. The organiser does not take out insurance for participants and accepts no liability arising from a participant’s lack, insufficiency or lapse of insurance. A medical check-up appropriate to the demands of the race is also recommended.

12. Assumption of risk and the organiser’s liability

Taking part in Ultra Trail Larre® means accepting the risks inherent in a self-supported, long-distance mountain race: among others, terrain, elevation, weather, wildlife, fatigue, dehydration, hypothermia or heatstroke, navigation, and the absence of assistance between controls. Participants accept these risks freely, knowingly and voluntarily on registering, and acknowledge that the organiser cannot remove them.

The organiser is liable for damage that is a direct result of a breach attributable to it — seriously defective marking, failure of the control system where it depends solely on the organiser, or a failure to meet safety measures that are its responsibility — within the limits of the applicable law, and does not exclude liability for its own wilful misconduct or gross negligence.

The organiser is not liable for damage arising from: the inherent sporting risks accepted under this article; force majeure or unforeseeable circumstances; a participant’s breach of these rules, the organiser’s instructions or safety rules; a participant’s own decisions during the race — continuing despite fatigue or injury, choices of gear or pace, navigation; or the acts of third parties unconnected with the organiser. It is never liable for indirect damage, loss of profit or non-material damage.

If, despite the above, some liability of the organiser towards a participant is established for events connected with the race, that liability is limited, to the extent permitted by applicable consumer law, to the entry fee paid by the affected participant.

13. Zero Waste Code

Whatever comes into the mountains with the runner leaves with the runner. Leaving any waste on the route is prohibited. A breach confirmed by the organiser or at a control is sanctioned with a time penalty, or, where the breach is serious or repeated, with disqualification.

14. Sporting conduct and doping

Fair play is required at all times, along with respect for other participants, for the people and livestock found on the route, and for the natural surroundings the race crosses. Receiving outside assistance not allowed under the self-sufficiency rules is prohibited, as is the use of substances or methods banned under the anti-doping rules applicable to the sport. The organiser may exclude or disqualify any participant currently serving a doping sanction imposed by a competent body.

15. Image rights

By registering, participants authorise the organiser, free of charge, to capture and use their image and voice taken during the race (photographs, video, results and ranking data) for informational, promotional and communication purposes relating to Ultra Trail Larre® and Musubigara®, on the website, on social media and in promotional materials, under Article 8.2.c) of Spanish Organic Law 1/1982. Anyone who does not wish to grant this authorisation must tell the organiser in writing before the race starts; declining does not prevent you from taking part, though the organiser cannot guarantee excluding your image from group shots of the course, the start or the finish.

16. Changes, postponement and cancellation of the race

The organiser may change the route, format or dates for reasons of safety, weather, administrative authorisation or force majeure, notifying registered participants. If the organiser cancels the race or postpones it to a later date, entry fees are not refunded in cash: each registered participant will instead receive a voucher for the full amount of their fee to take part in the next edition.

17. Voluntary withdrawal and refunds

The entry fee is not refundable in the event of a participant’s voluntary withdrawal, for any reason — including health, work or personal reasons — except as set out in Article 16 for cancellation or postponement decided by the organiser. Transferring a bib to another person is not allowed.

18. Complaints and disciplinary procedure

Results are published provisionally. Any complaint about the results or about how these rules have been applied must be submitted in writing to the organiser, through the official contact, within 15 days of the provisional results being published or of the event complained about. The organiser will issue a reasoned decision, which exhausts the internal sporting process, without prejudice to the participant’s right to go to court under Article 20.

19. Data protection and intellectual property

Personal data given at registration is processed in accordance with the privacy policy. The text, route, logos and other content of these rules and of the website, and the Ultra Trail Larre® brand, are governed by the legal notice.

20. Acceptance, changes to these rules and governing law

By completing registration, participants accept these rules in full. The organiser may publish updated versions of these rules before the race; the version in force is always the one published on this page, with the “Last updated” date shown at the top. These rules are governed by Spanish law. For any dispute, the parties submit to the courts of Vitoria-Gasteiz, unless consumer protection legislation establishes a different mandatory jurisdiction.
