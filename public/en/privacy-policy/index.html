Privacy policy

2. What data we process and why

We only process the data you give us, in three ways.

2.1. Pre-registration form. First name and surname, age, sex, email address, previous races (optional), whether you are a member of a mountaineering federation and which one, your personal description, comments (optional), the language of the page and the date and time of submission. We also generate a unique reference code. The purpose is to manage your pre-registration: validate it, allocate places in order of arrival, send you the confirmation and email you the payment date. We do not ask for your ID number at this stage; if you confirm your place, we will ask for it then and tell you so at that moment.

2.2. “Partner with us” form. Company or organisation, name, job title, email address, type of partnership and message. The purpose is to deal with your proposal and reply to you.

2.3. Contact by email or WhatsApp. Name, email address or phone number and the content of your message, to answer your enquiry.

Pre-registering does not reserve a bib, does not enter you in the race and does not subscribe you to any newsletter. We do not build profiles or take automated decisions.

3. Legal basis

Your express consent (Art. 6.1.a GDPR), given by ticking the box before sending each form or by writing to us. You can withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of the earlier processing. When your message seeks a partnership, we also apply pre-contractual measures at your request (Art. 6.1.b GDPR).

4. Recipients and international transfers

We do not pass your data to third parties unless legally required. We rely on providers acting as processors on our behalf:

Netlify, Inc. (United States): website hosting, the functions that process the forms and storage of submissions.
Resend, Inc. (United States): sending of confirmation and notification emails.
Google Ireland Limited: the email service from which we answer your messages.
Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd., only if you decide to write to us on WhatsApp or visit Instagram, under their own policy.

Hosting and email sending involve international transfers of data to the United States, covered by the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, depending on the provider. Fonts, images and other resources are served from our own domain, with no requests to third-party servers.

5. Retention

Pre-registration: until the race takes place, planned for autumn 2027, and then blocked for the limitation periods of possible liabilities. Deleted earlier if you withdraw your consent or request erasure.

Partner form and contact: for as long as your proposal or enquiry is being handled and then blocked for the statutory limitation periods.

6. Your rights

You may exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and portability, and withdraw your consent at any time, by writing to ignacio@musubigara.org stating the right you wish to exercise and your reference code, if you have one. If you believe your rights have not been respected, you may lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).

7. Cookies and analytics

This website does not set its own or third-party cookies and does not use advertising tools. The language preference is reflected only in the page address (/, /eu/ and /en/). If analytics or non-exempt cookies are added in the future, this policy will be updated and, where required, your prior consent will be requested.

8. Minors

Pre-registration and participation in the race are intended for adults. We do not knowingly process data of minors without the consent of their legal guardians.

9. Security and changes

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data: encrypted connections, keys kept out of the source code and restricted access to submissions. We may update this policy to reflect legal or website changes; the current version is always the one published here.
